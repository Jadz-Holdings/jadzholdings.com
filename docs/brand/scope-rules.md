1. Smart parking and vehicle-service coordination
2. Media, advertising, and digital engagement
3. Software platforms and interactive venue experiences

Use **Our Ventures** as the public portfolio label. Distinguish legal subsidiaries from brands, platforms, and products.

## Claim boundaries

Do not invent:

- Revenue, valuation, AUM, employee counts, customers, testimonials
- Patents, certifications, fundraising achievements
- Operating locations or launch dates beyond supplied mailing address context
- Extra executives, advisory boards, or fabricated biography details
- Partner logos or signed commercial relationships
- Response-time promises on contact

Development status must remain visible (“In development”). Do not imply that SPAAVS reservations, charging coordination, services, fleet integrations, or autonomous operations are currently available.

Present parking facilities as **partners**. Do not include mandatory facility-software subscriptions in the SPAAVS commercial model.

Keep confidential financial projections and internal planning notes off the public site.

Privacy and Terms pages are review drafts based on actual site configuration — do not invent certifications, retention periods, or contractual commitments.
