### SPAAVS

- **Display / a11y:** SPAAVS  
- **Wordmark:** SPΛΛVS  
- **Descriptor:** Smart Parking & Autonomous Vehicle Services  
- **Tagline:** Turning Parking Into Possibility.  
- **Status:** In development  
- **Button:** Explore SPAAVS → `/ventures/spaavs`

**Descriptions:**

1. SPAAVS is the trading name for Jadz Smart Parking and Autonomous Vehicle Services, a subsidiary of Jadz Holdings LLC. It is developing a platform that connects human drivers, parking facilities, vehicle-service providers, and fleet systems.  
2. The planned experience brings together parking discovery, reservations, EV charging, selected vehicle services, and unified billing. Compatible fleet and autonomous-vehicle integrations form part of its development direction.  
3. SPAAVS aims to work with existing parking facilities through partnerships, connecting available infrastructure with new service opportunities.

**Focus areas:** Parking discovery and reservations; EV charging coordination; Vehicle-service marketplace; Unified billing; Facility and fleet integrations; Future autonomous-ready capabilities at validated locations.

**Partnership model:** Facilities as partners — no mandatory facility-software subscriptions in the commercial model.  
**Disclaimer:** Capabilities are in development and not currently available as live services.
